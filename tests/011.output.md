Another day, another Red Ghost pitch

SINGLE_NEWLINE_P Actually I wrote this up last night, as one of the first comments on a “who should the ff villain be?” Reddit thread\. 

---

The Red Ghost and his Super Apes\.

They’re fun \(with the potential to be scary\) and they serve as a dark mirror of the heroes\. Importantly, though, they also work as rival adventurers\.

I am of the opinion that Fantastic Four should be structured like an Indiana Jones film \- instead of the plot revolving around the threat of the villain, it should be a race to some goal or mystery, and the villain should serve as a rival rather than the main plot motivator\. Fantastic Four is about adventure and discovery, not just “stopping the bad guy”, so I think that’s how they should approach the movie\.

Hence, Red Ghost, the scientist adventurer who wanted to beat the FF to the moon and replicate their origin\. Cast Mark Hamill or John Malkovich\.

If you wanted to take this further, you could throw in other parties as well\. Bring in the Wizard, the Mad Thinker, etc\., and have them all chasing after whatever scientific mystery or cosmic discovery the plot revolves around\. Use this movie to introduce those characters, then bring them back in later projects\.

\(Also, if possible, I’d love to see the Puppet Master\. I envision him as a Hannibal Lecter\-like figure manipulating events from a jail cell\. Cast Steve Buscemi\.\)

On a separate note, just for completeness, there are some commonly proposed villains that I don’t think should be in the first movie:

- ⁠Mole Man: I don’t think he’s the right brand of sci\-fi for the FF’s introductory, foundational movie\. I think the central pitch for the FF is a more cosmic/technology flavor of sci\-fi, and that should be the baseline this movie establishes\. Mole Man is a different, “Journey to the Center of the Earth” flavor \- save that for the sequel, as an expansion of what an FF story can be\.
- ⁠Super\-Skrull: The Skrulls need to already have a grievance against the FF in order to create the Super\-Skrull, which requires a prior encounter, which in turn requires a villainous faction of Skrulls with power and authority\. Marvel Studios simply haven’t yet laid the groundwork for this character\.
- ⁠Rama\-Tut: The FF have such a great, colorful rogues gallery that I’d hate to see them use up their first film on a crossover villain\. The film should have its own distinct, standalone identity, and the villain is part of that\. Introduce the FF on their own terms before pulling them into the Kang story\.
